Sensitivity Labels
~10 min
Why this mattersWithout labels, a file marked "Confidential" is just words in a filename. Sensitivity Labels bake the classification into the file itself - the label travels with the document wherever it goes, enforces encryption, and can block certain actions. Labels classify. Think of it as a tamper-evident seal that Microsoft 365 actually enforces.
1
Open the Purview portal

Go to purview.microsoft.com and sign in with your mcacloud.uk account.

Left nav → Solutions → Solution catalog → Information protection

This is the hub for sensitivity labels. From here you can access Labels, Label policies, and Auto-labelling. DLP and Data Lifecycle Management are separate solution cards.

2
Create a new Sensitivity Label

Click Labels then + Create a label. Fill in:

FieldValue
NameMCA Confidential
Display nameMCA Confidential
Description for usersInternal use only - do not share externally

Click Next.

3
Configure encryption and visual marking

On Scope, check Items. Click Next.

On Protection settings, tick Apply or remove encryption. Click Next.

On Encryption: assign permissions now, never expires, 7 days offline. Click Assign permissions, add your account with Co-Author rights, click Save.

On Content marking, toggle on Add a header. Set header text to CONFIDENTIAL - MCA INTERNAL in red.

Click through remaining pages and click Create label.

4
Publish the label via a policy

Click Label policies then Publish label. Select MCA Confidential. Choose All users and groups. Name the policy MCA Confidential Policy and submit.

⏱Label policies take up to 24 hours to appear in Office apps. They're visible in the portal immediately.
✅
Checkpoint 1

MCA Confidential label listed under Labels. MCA Confidential Policy listed under Label policies. Encryption and red header are configured.

✓
MCA Confidential label created and visible in Labels list
✓
Encryption configured with Co-Author permissions
✓
Label policy published to all users
2
Data Loss Prevention Policy
~10 min
Why this mattersA label classifies. DLP enforces. Without DLP a user could label something Confidential and still email it to their personal Gmail. DLP catches that and blocks it. The two work together: labels classify, DLP enforces.
1
Navigate to DLP policies
Left nav → Solutions → Solution catalog → Data Loss Prevention → Policies

Click + Create policy.

2
Use the UK Financial Data template

Select Financial then UK Financial Data. This pre-loads sensitive information types like UK bank account numbers, sort codes and credit cards. Name the policy MCA - Block UK Financial Data.

3
Set locations and action

Enable Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive, and Teams. Click Next.

Choose Create or customise advanced DLP rules. Edit the High volume rule - set action to Block everyone and enable Notify users with a policy tip.

4
Set to Test mode

Select Test it out first with policy tips enabled. Click Submit.

💡In a real deployment you run Test mode for 2-4 weeks, review matches, then flip to On. This avoids blocking legitimate work on day one.
✅
Checkpoint 2

DLP policy listed with status Test. Monitoring Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive and Teams for UK financial data. Logging matches and showing policy tips.

✓
DLP policy created using UK Financial Data template
✓
Policy covers Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive and Teams
✓
Running in Test mode with policy tips enabled
3
Retention Policy
~8 min
Why this mattersOrganisations must keep certain records for defined periods - GDPR, FCA rules, HMRC. Retention policies automate this. They also enforce deletion when you should NOT keep data beyond a certain point. This protects you both ways.
1
Navigate to Data Lifecycle Management
Left nav → Solutions → Solution catalog → Data Lifecycle Management → Retention policies

Click + New retention policy.

2
Configure the policy

Name it MCA - 7 Year Email Retention. Choose Static scope. Enable Exchange mailboxes only.

Set retention period to 7 years, starting from when items were created. At the end of the period: Delete items automatically.

💡Key exam distinction: a Retention Policy is broad (whole mailbox). A Retention Label is granular (specific item). If both exist and conflict, the most restrictive wins.
✅
Checkpoint 3

All Exchange email will be retained for 7 years from creation, then auto-deleted. A user deleting an email early just hides it - Purview keeps the copy.

✓
7-year retention policy created for Exchange mailboxes
✓
Policy set to delete automatically at end of retention period
4
eDiscovery and Legal Hold
~8 min
Why this mattersHR flags an employee for misconduct. Legal need all their emails and Teams messages as evidence - fast - without them being able to delete anything. The Legal Hold overrides everything, including retention policies and user deletions. The data is frozen until the hold is removed.
⚠Permission check: You must be individually added to the eDiscovery Manager role group before this section. Go to Settings → Role groups → eDiscovery Manager and confirm your account is listed. Note: security groups cannot be added here - individual accounts only.
1
Create an eDiscovery case
Left nav → Solutions → Solution catalog → Data Security Investigations → eDiscovery (Standard)

Click + Create a case. Name it MCA Investigation 001. Click Save.

2
Apply a Legal Hold

Open the case. Click Hold tab then + Create. Name it MCA Hold 001.

Add your own mailbox as the test subject. Leave the query blank (hold everything). Click Create this hold.

⚠A Legal Hold is indefinite by default. It supersedes any retention policy or user deletion. On the exam: Legal Hold always wins.
3
Run a Content Search

Click the Searches tab, then + New search. Name it MCA Search 001. Add your mailbox as the location. Enter confidential as the keyword. Click Submit. When complete, click Review sample to see matched items.

✅
Checkpoint 4

Live case with a Legal Hold protecting the mailbox. Even if the user deletes emails now, the held copies are preserved in the compliance store.

✓
eDiscovery case created
✓
Legal Hold applied to a mailbox
✓
Content search run and results visible
5
Audit Logs
~5 min
Why this mattersEverything you've done in this lab has been logged. If someone bypasses a DLP policy, changes a retention setting, or accesses a file they shouldn't - it's in the audit log. Audit Standard: 90 days. Audit Premium: 1 year. ISO 27001 and PCI DSS often require a minimum of 1 year.
1
Search your own activity
Left nav → Solutions → Solution catalog → Audit

Set date range to Today. In the Users field enter your own account. Leave Activities blank. Click Search.

2
Inspect a record

Click any result. Check these fields:

FieldWhat it tells you
DateExact timestamp of the action
UserWho did it (UPN)
ActivityWhat they did
IP addressWhere they were
ItemWhat they did it to

This record is immutable. An attacker with admin access cannot delete audit logs - they are write-only from the portal.

✅
Checkpoint 5

Your own activity is logged, timestamped, attributed to your account, and cannot be tampered with via the portal.

✓
Audit log search run for your own account today
✓
Individual record inspected and fields identified

Lab Complete - Exam Cheat Sheet

What you built and why it matters

ToolWhat it doesExam trigger
Sensitivity LabelClassifies and protects a file. Travels with it. Enforces encryption and markings."classify", "protect content", "encrypt a file"
DLP PolicyDetects sensitive data and blocks or warns before it leaves the org."prevent data leaving", "block email", "policy tip"
Retention PolicyKeeps or deletes content at location level on a schedule."keep for X years", "all email"
Retention LabelKeep or delete a specific item. Overrides a policy if more restrictive."specific document", "records management"
Legal HoldFreezes content indefinitely. Overrides everything."court case", "preserve evidence"
eDiscoverySearches across M365 for specific content."search mailboxes", "find all messages"
AuditImmutable log of who did what. 90 days Standard, 1 year Premium."who accessed", "admin log", "90 days"
🎯
The one thing to remember: Labels classify. DLP enforces. Retention keeps or deletes. Legal Hold overrides everything. Audit proves it all happened.
