Lab 4 of 6 Domain 2 - Authentication and Access Management P1 / P2 Licence

Conditional Access Policy (Report-Only) + Named Location + PIM Role Activation

SC-300 Microsoft Identity and Access Administrator - Hands-On Lab

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Create a Conditional Access policy in report-only mode
  • Understand CA policy conditions and grant controls
  • Create a named location for use in CA policies
  • Activate a PIM-eligible role assignment
  • Review the PIM audit log

✅ Pre-Requisites

  • Signed in with your student account
  • Your instructor has made you PIM-eligible for a role (e.g. Security Reader)
  • Entra ID P1 licence is available in the tenant (included in the M365 E5 dev tenant)

🛠️ Lab Steps

📋 Part 1 - Create a Named Location

  1. Navigate to Entra ID > Protection > Conditional Access > Named locations
  2. Click + IP ranges location
  3. Name it Lab - Trusted Office Range
  4. Add an IP range - use 192.168.1.0/24 as a fictional office range
  5. Check Mark as trusted location
  6. Click Create
  7. Exam tip: Named locations can be IP ranges or countries. Trusted locations bypass MFA when used in CA policy conditions. Country-based locations use the IP-to-country mapping at sign-in time.

📋 Part 2 - Create a Conditional Access Policy in Report-Only Mode

  1. Navigate to Entra ID > Protection > Conditional Access > Policies > + New policy
  2. Name the policy LAB - Require MFA for All Users (Report Only)
  3. Under Users, select All users
  4. Under Target resources, select All cloud apps
  5. Under Conditions > Locations, set Configure to Yes, include Any location, exclude All trusted locations
  6. Under Grant, select Require multifactor authentication and click Select
  7. Under Enable policy, select Report-only
  8. Click Create
  9. Exam tip: Report-only mode logs what the policy WOULD have done without enforcing it. Always pilot with report-only and review the sign-in logs before turning a policy On.

📋 Part 3 - Review the Policy in Sign-In Logs

  1. Navigate to Entra ID > Monitoring & health > Sign-in logs
  2. Find a recent sign-in entry and click on it
  3. Click the Conditional Access tab
  4. Find your lab policy in the list - it should show as Report-only: Would have applied or similar
  5. Review the Conditions matched and Grant controls applied columns
  6. Exam tip: The sign-in log CA tab shows all policies that evaluated for that sign-in, their result, and why. This is the primary troubleshooting tool for CA issues in the exam.

📋 Part 4 - Activate a PIM Role

  1. Navigate to Entra ID > Identity Governance > Privileged Identity Management > My roles
  2. Click Entra ID roles and find your eligible role assignment
  3. Click Activate next to the role
  4. Set the Duration to 1 hour
  5. Enter a Reason: SC-300 lab exercise
  6. Click Activate and wait for confirmation
  7. Return to My roles > Active assignments and confirm the role is now active
  8. Exam tip: PIM activation can require MFA, an approval, or a justification - all are configurable in the role settings. Know which of these are enforced by which PIM policy settings.

📋 Part 5 - Review PIM Audit Log

  1. Navigate to PIM > Entra ID roles > Audit history
  2. Find your activation event in the log
  3. Review the Requestor, Action, Resource, and Time fields
  4. Note that PIM audit logs are available for 30 days in the portal and can be exported to Log Analytics for longer retention
  5. Deactivate your role when finished: PIM > My roles > Active assignments > Deactivate
