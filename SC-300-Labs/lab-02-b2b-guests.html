Invite and Manage B2B Guest Users

SC-300 Microsoft Identity and Access Administrator - Hands-On Lab

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Invite an external user as a B2B guest
  • Understand the guest invitation and redemption flow
  • Review and configure External Collaboration Settings
  • Assign a guest user to a group
  • Remove a guest user from the tenant

✅ Pre-Requisites

  • Signed in with your User Administrator student account
  • A personal email address you can access (Gmail, Outlook.com, or similar) to act as the external guest
  • Access to that personal email inbox to redeem the invitation

🛠️ Lab Steps

📋 Part 1 - Review External Collaboration Settings

  1. Navigate to Entra ID > External Identities > External collaboration settings
  2. Review Guest user access restrictions - note the three options (same as member users, limited directory access, most restricted)
  3. Review Guest invite settings - confirm who can invite guests
  4. Review Collaboration restrictions - note you can allowlist or blocklist specific domains
  5. Exam tip: Know what each guest access restriction level means. SC-300 regularly tests the difference between the three access levels and what a guest can see in each.
  6. Do not change any settings - note them for your understanding only

📋 Part 2 - Invite a Guest User

  1. Navigate to Entra ID > Users > All Users > + New User > Invite external user
  2. Enter your personal email address in the Email field
  3. Set Display name to External Guest Test
  4. Add a personal message if you wish, then click Invite
  5. Confirm the guest appears in All Users with User type: Guest

📋 Part 3 - Redeem the Invitation

  1. Open your personal email inbox and find the invitation email from Microsoft
  2. Click Accept invitation - you will be taken to a consent page
  3. Accept the permissions request - you are now a guest in the mcacloud.uk tenant
  4. Exam tip: Until a guest redeems their invitation, their account shows as PendingAcceptance in Entra ID. The exam tests this status.
  5. Return to your student account and check the guest user - confirm invitation state has changed

📋 Part 4 - Assign Guest to a Group

  1. Navigate to Entra ID > Groups > All Groups
  2. Find or create a test group (+ New Group, Security, name it Lab Test Group)
  3. Open the group and navigate to Members > + Add members
  4. Search for your guest user and add them
  5. Confirm they appear as a member of the group

📋 Part 5 - Remove the Guest

  1. Return to All Users, find your guest account and click Delete
  2. Confirm deletion - this removes their access to your tenant but does not delete their home account
  3. Exam tip: Deleting a B2B guest from your tenant only removes their access to YOUR tenant. Their home Microsoft account is unaffected.
