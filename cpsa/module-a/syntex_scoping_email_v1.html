Client Details
Test Window
"Normal business hours" needs to be defined precisely. Standard UK business hours are 09:00-17:30 Monday to Friday. Always specify timezone too.
Network Scope
The email gives two different IP ranges (10.10.0.0/16 and 192.168.1.0/24). Neither can be confirmed without speaking to Dave in networking (who is on holiday). The correct answer is to flag this as TBC / conflicting - never guess.
Out-of-Scope Items (Scope Creep)
Three items require separate scoping: (1) AWS customer portal - cloud scope needs formal cloud-specific agreement, (2) phishing / social engineering - requires separate legal sign-off and defined target list, (3) MD's personal laptop - endpoint not on the server estate, separate engagement.
