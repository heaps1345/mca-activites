Client Engagement Brief

About Syntex Solutions Ltd

Syntex Solutions Ltd is a Manchester-based financial technology company providing payment processing, digital banking infrastructure, and regulatory compliance software to mid-market financial institutions across the UK and EU. The company employs approximately 280 staff and processes in excess of GBP 400 million in transactions annually.

Syntex Solutions is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) under reference number 987654 and is subject to Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) v4.0 compliance requirements. The company holds ISO 27001:2022 certification for its information security management system.

Reason for Engagement

Syntex Solutions has commissioned My Cyber Academy to conduct an internal penetration test of its server infrastructure prior to an annual PCI DSS audit. The engagement forms part of the company's ongoing security assurance programme and satisfies Requirement 11.4 of PCI DSS v4.0 (penetration testing).

The Head of IT, Mr James Elliott, has raised concerns that legacy Linux infrastructure may contain unpatched vulnerabilities following a period of delayed maintenance. The Windows domain environment has not been subject to security review since its initial deployment.

Engagement Objectives

  • Identify exploitable vulnerabilities across in-scope infrastructure
  • Assess the risk of unauthorised access to financial data and cardholder data
  • Evaluate Active Directory security posture and privilege escalation paths
  • Provide a risk-rated findings report suitable for PCI DSS audit evidence
  • Deliver remediation guidance prioritised by business risk

Rules of Engagement

Permitted Activities

  • Network scanning and enumeration of in-scope IP addresses
  • Vulnerability identification using automated and manual techniques
  • Exploitation of identified vulnerabilities on in-scope systems only
  • Privilege escalation on in-scope systems
  • Credential testing using identified or brute-forced credentials on in-scope systems
  • Interception and analysis of network traffic within the lab environment
  • Creation of test accounts and files for evidence purposes (to be removed post-engagement)

Prohibited Activities

  • Testing of any system not listed in the in-scope IP ranges
  • Denial of Service attacks or any action likely to cause system instability or data loss
  • Exfiltration of real personal data or financial records
  • Modification or deletion of data not created by the tester during this engagement
  • Testing outside the agreed testing window without written approval
  • Sharing of credentials, findings, or access with any third party
  • Pivoting from in-scope systems to out-of-scope systems

Critical Finding Procedure

If a critical vulnerability is discovered that poses an immediate risk to business operations or cardholder data, the tester must:

  1. Immediately cease exploitation of that specific vulnerability
  2. Notify the emergency contact by phone within 4 hours
  3. Document the finding with timestamp and evidence
  4. Await written instruction before resuming testing of that system
  5. Continue testing other in-scope systems where safe to do so
