01. Engagement Brief - Read Before Proceeding

Confidential REF: MCA-CPSA-2024-E01  |  Penetration Test Authorisation

Client Overview

Syntex Solutions Ltd is a UK-based financial technology company providing payment processing and digital banking services to retail and SME customers. Syntex processes cardholder data and is subject to PCI DSS v4.0, UK GDPR, and FCA SYSC regulatory obligations.

Syntex has engaged My Cyber Academy to conduct an internal infrastructure penetration test of their Windows domain environment, with a focus on identifying attack paths that could lead to Domain Admin compromise or exposure of cardholder data.

Rules of Engagement

  • Testing window: Monday to Friday, 08:00 - 18:00 UK time
  • Denial of service attacks are strictly prohibited
  • Do not exfiltrate real cardholder data beyond what is necessary to evidence a finding
  • All commands and output must be logged for inclusion in the final report
  • If you gain Domain Admin access, stop and document - do not make changes to AD
  • Findings must be reported using the CVSS v3.1 scoring framework
  • Any critical finding (CVSS 9.0+) must be verbally reported to the engagement lead immediately

Your Role

You are acting as a CREST-authorised penetration tester conducting an authorised assessment. You have been provided with an internal network position (Kali Linux VM) with layer-2 access to the Syntex internal network segment. No credentials have been provided - this is a grey-box assessment starting from network access only.
